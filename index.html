Interpretive cautions (from Discussion & Limitations)

• Long prompt differs in specificity and reasoning instructions, not only length — factor is “increasingly detailed and structured prompting”.
• Response length also grew (32 → 85 → 126 words); verbosity bias cannot be separated.
• Fixed presentation order, visible condition labels, same chat session → order, carry-over and expectation effects possible.
• Accuracy is perceived (event unverifiable). Effect sizes are small (Kendall’s W .05–.08; pairwise r .25–.39).
• Phase 1 respondents used own, unrecorded AI tools; ER reliabilities and criterion weights are assumptions. Relevance is flat (p=.996).
